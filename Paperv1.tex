\section{Introduction}
Heatmaps (Grad-CAM, SHAP, etc.) appear in most medical imaging AI 
papers and are presented as reassurance that the model can be trusted. 

*briefly explain the methods 

Thesis:Saliency explanations in medical imaging are mostly checked for plausibility (does it look right, do users like it) and not for faithfulness (does it reflect the model's computation). Higher user ratings don't mean users trust the model for the right reasons.  

Key idea: separate what a paper {claims} the heatmap shows 
from what it actually tests.  

A heatmap used as a decision aid 
needs a user study with wrong AI cases. A heatmap used as evidence of 
what the model relies on needs faithfulness tests. 

Contributions (three): (1) a framework separating faithfulness, 
plausibility and simulatability; (2) an audit of recent papers; 
(3) a minimum reporting checklist.
